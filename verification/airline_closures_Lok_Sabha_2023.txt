                OPERATIONAL DOMESTIC AIRLINES
1337.    SHRI DHANUSH M. KUMAR
         SHRI C.N. ANNADURAI
         SHRI SELVAM G.
         SHRIMATI MANJULATA MANDAL
Will the Minister of CIVIL AVIATION
                                             be pleased to state:-
(a) the details of domestic airlines operating in the country, their
market share, the time since operating along with number of
employees engaged by each domestic airline;

(b) the number of domestic airlines functioning in Tamil Nadu
alongwith number of air passenger who have travelled in these
domestic airlines during last three years;

(c) whether any of these airlines has discontinued operations and if
so, the details thereof along with number of employees rendered
jobless by each of these airlines;

(d) whether most of the domestic airlines are incurring losses and
profit earned has also drastically reduced during each of the last
three years and if so, the details therefor and if not, the reasons
therefor; and

(e) whether the Government has any proposal to give incentives to
domestic airlines for operating to and from certain regional and
remote areas and if so, the details thereof along with remote areas
to be connected with air routes?
                               ANSWER
Minister of State in the Ministry of CIVIL AVIATION                  (GEN.
(DR) V. K. SINGH (RETD))
(a):The details of the domestic airlines which have been issued Air
Operator   Certificate     (AOC)   by   Directorate   General   of    Civil
Aviation(DGCA) to start the operations in the country are at
Annexure-I. The details of market share of the Scheduled Indian
Airlines are at Annexure- II. Number of Personnel of Scheduled Indian
Airlines during last three years are at Annexure-III.

(b): The number of domestic airlines functioning in Tamil Nadu along
with number of air passengers (Domestic and International) is at
Annexure - IV.

(c): List of the Airlines which discontinued operations during last 5
years is at Annexure-V.;
;
(d): Details of the Financial Performance of the Scheduled Indian
Airlines during last three years are at Annexure-VI.;
;
(e): Ministry of Civil Aviation launched Regional Connectivity Scheme
(RCS) - UDAN (Ude Desh ka Aam Nagrik) on 21-10-2016 to stimulate
regional air connectivity and make air travel affordable for the
masses. The UDAN scheme envisages providing connectivity to
unserved and underserved airports of the country through revival of
the existing airstrips and airports. Financial Support to the Selected
Airline Operators is in the form of Viability Gap Funding (VGF). State
Governments provide 20% share towards VGF for RCS flights
pertaining to their states. However, the share of VGF for North-
Eastern states and the Union Territories is 10%.
